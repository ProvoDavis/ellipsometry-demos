The film–substrate coefficients r23 use the same forms with 1 → 2 and 2 → 3. Complex indices are written Ñ = n − ik. With these signs Rp = −Rs at normal incidence, so Δ = 180° there, and for bare silicon Δ stays near 180° below its Brewster angle. SiO₂ does not absorb at 632.8 nm, so β is real and every curve repeats after one film period D = λ / (2N2 cos φ2).
