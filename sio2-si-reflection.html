The film–substrate coefficients r23 use the same forms with 1 → 2 and 2 → 3. Complex indices are written Ñ = n − ik. With these signs Rp = −Rs at normal incidence, so Δ = 180° there. SiO₂ does not absorb anywhere from 1.5 to 6 eV, so β is real and, at a fixed energy, every curve repeats after one film period D = λ / (2N2 cos φ2).

Optical constants: SiO₂ from Malitson's Sellmeier formula for fused silica (1965); Si from Aspnes and Studna (1983), tabulated every 0.1 eV from 1.5 to 6 eV and interpolated smoothly. Both come from refractiveindex.info. At 632.8 nm (1.959 eV) they give N2 = 1.457 and Ñ3 = 3.881 − 0.019i.
